How Muster works

Muster is a website where community groups run fundraising events and the public can support them. Every dollar goes to the charity that the event was created for.

1

Create an event

A community group creates a fundraising event, chooses a charity and sets a goal.

2

Share it

The organiser shares the page and people register their interest in coming along.

3

Donate

Supporters donate online and receive a tax receipt by email straight away.
