Coorparoo P&C Biggest Morning Tea

Thursday 21 May 2026 - Coorparoo State School Hall, Coorparoo QLD 4151

About the organiser

Coorparoo State School P&C Association
Community group - Coorparoo QLD - member since 2021 - 6 events run

We have run a morning tea in the school hall every May for eleven years. It started with one teacher, two urns and a card table. This year the whole staff room bakes and the Year 6 students run the door. All money raised goes to Cancer Council Queensland.

Money goes to: Cancer Council Queensland (ABN 48 321 126 727). This charity is a deductible gift recipient, so donations of $2 or more are tax deductible.

About this event

Doors open at 9:30am in the school hall. Entry is a gold coin and a slice of cake costs a bit more. There is also a raffle on the day with prizes donated by local businesses.

If you cannot come on the day you can still donate on this page. Every donation appears on the tally board in the hall so the students can watch the total go up.

Our fundraising goal

Our goal is $8,000. This would pay for around 320 calls to the Cancer Council support line, or 26 nights of accommodation for a country family staying in Brisbane for treatment.
